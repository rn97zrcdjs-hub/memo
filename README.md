# Memo

**Pitch**

> Your agent vendor can tell you a conversation went wrong; we're the independent check that tells you *which article* made it wrong and fixes it, so future answers will always come out right.

**Our weakest point, and what I'd say when a prospect finds it**

The agent vendors are building groundedness scoring themselves (like Decagon). Once grading is built in, "we grade your conversations" stops being enough. When a prospect raises it:

> "Good, use it. When it flags a bad answer, ask it two questions: which article caused this, and who fixes it? Their QA grades their own agent and stops at the conversation. We're the second opinion and the fix, and we stay with you if you add a second agent or switch vendors."

The honest answer is that our defensible ground is independence plus remediation, not grading alone. We should stop leading with grading.

**The one number for the weekly dashboard**

**The Delta:** the share of graded customer answers, across every live customer, that no current source supports. It's the product's promise (Delta Zero) as a number, so sales, product and deployment all move it.

**What I'd want to exist to do this at 10x**

**An automatic "trace pack."** Point it at a prospect's public help center plus a handful of their conversations, and it produces the the story before the second meeting: the worst wrong answer, the exact article that caused it, and why that article is wrong. Today that's the motion that wins, and it's done by hand. Automated, I could send one to every qualified prospect, and give it to our BPO partners to open doors for us.
